### Kya "Amazon Textract Medical" Koi Alag Service Hai? ❌

Nahi! **"Amazon Textract Medical" naam ki koi alag service AWS mein exist hi nahi karti.**

Exam ke andar is tarah ke options sirf students ko confuse karne ke liye banaye jate hain (yeh aik **distractor** hota hai).

---

### Asal Mein AWS Ki Services Alag-Alag Hain: 🔍

1. **Amazon Textract (Regular Service):**
* Yeh aik aisi service hai jo kisi bhi PDF, scanned document, ya image se text, tables, aur forms ko extract (bahir nikalne) ke liye use hoti hai (chahe document medical ho ya financial).


2. **Amazon Comprehend Medical:**
* Yeh aik alag **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** service hai jo medical text ko parhti hai aur uske andar se **PHI (Protected Health Information)** ya sensitive medical data ko detect karti hai.



---

### Sahi Jora (Combination) Kya Hai? 💡

Sawal mein jab yeh pucha gaya ke PDF reports se text bhi nikalna hai aur PHI bhi detect karni hai, toh uska sahi jawab yeh banta hai:

* **Amazon Textract** (text extract karne ke liye) + **Amazon Comprehend Medical** (PHI detect karne ke liye).

Isi wajah se woh option jisme "Amazon Textract Medical" likha tha, woh bilkul ghalat (fake) option tha!
